Delete your RepForgeAI account

You can permanently delete your RepForgeAI account and associated account data directly from the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open RepForgeAI.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to the Danger Zone.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm deletion.
Account deletion is permanent. You will need to create a new account if you want to use account-based features again later.

What is deleted

When the in-app deletion flow completes successfully, RepForgeAI removes the account and associated remote user data that the app manages for that account, including:

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • Cloud-synced workout sessions and deleted-session records
  • Community public profile
  • Community posts created by the account
  • Kudos and related in-app Community notification data tied to the account where applicable
  • Account-scoped Community block and policy-acceptance records
  • Reports submitted by the account
  • Local RepForgeAI user data stored by the app on that device as part of the completed deletion flow

Moderation and safety records

Some abuse, safety, fraud-prevention, legal, or moderation records may need to be retained when reasonably necessary for those purposes. RepForgeAI does not treat those records as active Community content.

If you cannot access the app

You can request account deletion by contacting RepForgeAI support from the same email address associated with your account. The support address must be added here before this page is published.
